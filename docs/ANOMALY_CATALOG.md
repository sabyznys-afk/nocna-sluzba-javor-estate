# NOČNÁ ZMENA: BLOK 14 — *Archív anomálií* (32-Anomaly Catalog)

Every anomaly belongs to one of 5 classifications and supports up to four player actions:
`[1] Log & Report` | `[2] Contain / Stabilize` | `[3] Investigate Closer` | `[4] Ignore`

---

## Category I: Architectural & Spatial Anomalies (7)
1. **ANOM_01 — *Trináste okno* (The Thirteenth Window)** *(Min Night 1 | Courtyard East Wall)*: An extra dark window appears on the second floor where solid brick exists on daytime blueprints. Looking away causes faint tapping from the glass.
2. **ANOM_02 — *Predĺžená pavlač* (Endless Gallery)** *(Min Night 2 | Pavlač)*: The 2nd-floor wooden balcony stretches endlessly until the player kills their flashlight and walks backward three paces.
3. **ANOM_03 — *Nesprávne dvere* (Mismatched Threshold)** *(Min Night 3 | Flat 9)*: Opening Flat 9's apartment door reveals the steam-choked Boiler Room stairs instead of a hallway.
4. **ANOM_04 — *Chýbajúci schod* (Ghost Step)** *(Min Night 1 | Attic Stairs)*: An extra 14th stone step manifests at the attic landing; stepping on it produces wet splash audio instead of stone.
5. **ANOM_05 — *Zazděná brána* (Bricked Exit)** *(Min Night 4 | Main Gate)*: For 20 seconds after 03:00, the wrought-iron street gate is replaced by seamless damp plaster bearing 1953 chalk marks.
6. **ANOM_06 — *Zrkadlový byt* (Mirrored Flat 7)** *(Min Night 2 | Flat 7)*: Inside Flat 7, furniture shifts cleanly to the opposite wall while the bathroom mirror reflects the corridor behind you with the door latched.
7. **ANOM_07 — *Čierna voda* (Black Well Water)** *(Min Night 1 | Flat 7 Sink & Courtyard Well)*: Dry medieval well stones drip thick, soot-black liquid upward into the courtyard fog.

## Category II: CCTV-Only Anomalies (6)
*Observed via the Vrátnica CRT monitor (`CAM-01`–`CAM-04`). Logging these earns progress toward `Slepá škvrna`.*
8. **ANOM_08 — *Dvojník pri vrátnici* (Booth Doppelgänger)** *(Min Night 1 | CAM-03)*: A figure in Marek's exact blue SBS uniform stands motionless outside the booth glass — invisible when looking directly out the window.
9. **ANOM_09 — *Denné vysielanie 1953* (1953 Daylight Feed)** *(Min Night 3 | CAM-02)*: Camera 2 shows the courtyard bathed in overcast 1953 afternoon light while men in trench coats haul crates downstairs.
10. **ANOM_10 — *Prázdna šachta* (Open Well Lid)** *(Min Night 2 | CAM-01)*: On CCTV, the concrete cap on the medieval well slides aside and wet hands grip the rim; stepping into the courtyard reveals the padlock untouched.
11. **ANOM_11 — *Škodovka bez šoféra* (Phantom Favorit Lights)** *(Min Night 2 | CAM-01)*: High beams on the parked Škoda Favorit flash Morse code (`50.4`) only on the monochrome feed.
12. **ANOM_12 — *Postava na zábradlí* (Gallery Rail Watcher)** *(Min Night 3 | CAM-02)*: A tall elongated silhouette leans over the 2nd-floor gallery railing staring directly into Camera 2's lens.
13. **ANOM_13 — *Dátum na páske* (Corrupted VCR Timestamp)** *(Min Night 4 | CAM-04)*: Camera 4's overlay flips rapidly between `16.11.1995` and `16.11.1953` as a figure drags a canvas sack through the Passage.

## Category III: Acoustic, Clock & Broadcast Anomalies (7)
14. **ANOM_14 — *Mŕtva linka* (Dead Payphone Ring)** *(Min Night 3 | Passage Payphone)*: An unplugged coin payphone rings piercingly at 03:00; answering plays Elena's plea layered beneath a 1953 ŠtB tape.
15. **ANOM_15 — *Spätný chod hodín* (Backward Watchmaker Clocks)** *(Min Night 2 | Hodinárstvo)*: Every pendulum and wall clock behind the Watchmaker's shutter ticks counterclockwise in unison until `02:14`.
16. **ANOM_16 — *Mŕtvy kanál v bytoch* (Vacant Flat TV Bleed)** *(Min Night 3 | Pavlač Flats)*: Disconnected apartments glow cold Channel-3 blue and blast deafening 50Hz static even with corridor breakers pulled.
17. **ANOM_17 — *Ihličková tlačiareň* (1954 Eviction Printout)** *(Min Night 2 | Notary Office)*: An unplugged dot-matrix printer clatters furiously, spitting endless eviction lists ending in Marek's own name.
18. **ANOM_18 — *Frekvencia 50.4 Hz* (Hidden Radio Broadcast)** *(Min Night 2 | 5 Hidden Radios)*: Pocket transistor radios tucked around Blok 14 hum old 1950s radio plays interrupted by whispered names of former tenants.
19. **ANOM_19 — *Ozvena pečiatky* (Delayed Clock Stamp)** *(Min Night 3 | Watchman's Clocks)*: Stamping a checkpoint clock triggers a second metallic stamp three seconds later from an empty corner.
20. **ANOM_20 — *Telefón zo dňa 00* (Self-Dialing Booth Phone)** *(Min Night 4 | Vrátnica)*: Your own rotary booth phone rings with no external line active; lifting the receiver plays Marek's own voice from earlier in the shift.

## Category IV: Time-Slip & Weather Anomalies (6)
21. **ANOM_21 — *Sneh do neba* (Upward Snowfall)** *(Min Night 5 | Courtyard)*: Pale ash-like November snow rises steadily from the damp cobblestones toward the night sky.
22. **ANOM_22 — *Bzučiaca lampa* (Staring Sodium Lamp)** *(Min Night 1 | Courtyard Center)*: The overhead sodium lamp dies instantly whenever looked at directly, buzzing back to amber only in peripheral vision.
23. **ANOM_23 — *Zaschnutá krv z 1953* (Weeping Plaster Names)** *(Min Night 4 | Bunker K-4 Corridor)*: Damp concrete walls sweat rusty condensation spelling archived case numbers (`K-4/112`, `K-4/118`).
24. **ANOM_24 — *Anténny oblúk* (STA Corona Discharge)** *(Min Night 3 | Rooftop STA Mast)*: The rooftop television mast crackles with pale blue static arcs aligned toward the courtyard well.
25. **ANOM_25 — *Cudzí dych v kotolni* (Freezing Steam)** *(Min Night 4 | Kotolňa)*: Pressurized boiler steam pipes frost over with rime ice despite the roaring furnace gauge showing 140°C.
26. **ANOM_26 — *Okno do roku 1953* (1953 Time-Slip Aperture)** *(Night 6 / NG+ | Courtyard & STA)*: Complete phase-shift exposing Blok 14 in November 1953 — fresh brick, ŠtB sentries, and unsealed concrete.

30. **ANOM_30 — *Ruka z kanála* (Well Grate Reacher)** *(Min Night 4 | Medieval Well)*: Faint static fingers press upward through the well grate whenever distortion exceeds 60%.
31. **ANOM_31 — *Svedok bez tváre* (Static Silhouette in Flat 4 Hall)** *(Min Night 5 | Pavlač)*: A faceless grey-coat figure standing silently outside Pani Mária's chain-locked door, guarding or waiting.
32. **ANOM_32 — *Rezonátor K-4* (Device K-4 Core Manifestation)** *(Min Night 4 | Bunker K-4)*: The sentient acoustic core inside the sub-cellar well, pulsing at 50Hz and warping color into raw phosphor monochrome.
